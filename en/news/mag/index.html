The mental health of deminers: results of the work with MAG

A partnership with the Ukrainian branch of the Mines Advisory Group since February 2024. In May the MAG team in Mykolaiv survived an airstrike.

May 26, 2025

An important distinction

The material in this section describes previous projects of the founders and team, including UCARE. It is not a programme, result or report of the UNIQ legal entity and does not confirm anyone’s current role at the UNIQ Foundation. Cases and experience →

Who, and since when

Work with the Ukrainian branch of The Mines Advisory Group (MAG) began on 9 February 2024.

MAG clears mines from de-occupied territory. It is work in which the cost of a mistake is not negotiable and the daily load on the mind has no pauses.

What happened in May

The MAG team in Mykolaiv survived an airstrike. The foundation's psychologists provided crisis support on site, not remotely.

That distinction matters. Crisis support a few days later and through a screen is a different service from being present in the first hours.

What we did next

We developed a series of psychological first aid trainings for MAG staff.

The logic is the same as in the veterans stream: the colleague working alongside you is available in the first minutes and a specialist is not. Teaching that colleague the basics of support covers the gap nobody else can.
