News

Foundation news and the stories of people going through recovery.

  • November 10, 2025

    The start of cooperation with SGPP

    The Swiss Society for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy funds 165 free consultations for Ukrainians.

  • May 26, 2025

    The mental health of deminers: results of the work with MAG

    A partnership with the Ukrainian branch of the Mines Advisory Group since February 2024. In May the MAG team in Mykolaiv survived an airstrike.

  • July 24, 2024

    From 4 to 87: portraits of the youngest and the oldest beneficiaries

    The platform's youngest client is four years old and the oldest is eighty-seven. Between them lies the whole war.

  • July 22, 2024

    More than 2,000 Ukrainians will receive free psychological help

    The result of the collaboration with German Doctors — by the end of the year.

  • July 22, 2024

    Psychological support for older people — with thanks to Medair

    A group that usually comes last in the queue for attention: older people who have lived through the war.

  • May 17, 2023

    EQ.app gains ground on Google Play

    One hundred thousand downloads on the store, a 4.4 rating and more than 1,200 reviews.

  • May 3, 2023

    The EQ.app team at Web Summit

    Taking part in Europe's largest technology conference as a national ambassador for Ukraine.

  • February 16, 2023

    EQ.app in the VentureWell business incubator

    A six-month GIST incubation programme in the United States.

  • January 5, 2023

    More than 1,000 consultations since the project began

    The platform's first milestone, broken down: who delivered the help, who received it, and what share were displaced.

  • January 5, 2023

    The Heroes project resumes its work

    The complex rehabilitation programme for demobilised soldiers returns, on a methodology developed back in 2016.
