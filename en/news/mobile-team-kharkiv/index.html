The mobile team started visiting front-line communities in Kharkiv oblast

Together with the NGO Dzherelo Nadii the foundation began regular visits by two specialists to eight communities with no psychologist within reach.

2026-06-12

How it works

Since June 2026 the foundation's mobile team has visited eight communities in Kharkiv oblast on a fixed schedule: each community once a fortnight, on the same weekday. Consistency matters more here than frequency. Someone who knows the specialist will be in the community on Thursday can plan to come; someone who does not know when the next visit is does not come at all.

Each visit is staffed by a psychologist and a social worker. They see people in rooms the community provides: usually a library, an outpatient clinic or a room in the council building. There is one requirement — a separate room with a door that closes, where a conversation cannot be heard in the corridor.

The partnership

The visits are run jointly with the NGO Dzherelo Nadii, which has worked in the region since 2022. The partner handles logistics, agreements with communities and initial outreach; the foundation provides the specialists, the methodology and supervision.

The division is not arbitrary: an organisation the community has known for years opens doors that "people from Lviv" do not.

The first three months

  • 46 field days;
  • 612 consultations, 128 of them repeat visits;
  • 71 people referred to the foundation's online service to continue work between visits;
  • 9 cases referred to psychiatric services — the foundation does not treat conditions requiring medication.

What proved difficult

The hardest part is not logistics but attrition between visits. A fortnight is too long for someone in an acute state. The partial answer is online sessions in between, but that only works where there is a stable connection and somewhere to speak alone. In the next period the foundation will test a weekly schedule in the three busiest communities.
