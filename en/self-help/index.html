Self-help

Reading for people who are not contacting anyone yet. What happens after a traumatic event, what can be done about it alone, and where alone stops being enough.

If you need help right now

7333

Lifeline Ukraine — suicide prevention, 24/7

  • 1547Government hotline — domestic and gender-based violence, 24/7
  • 116 111National child helpline, daily 12:00–20:00

These lines operate around the clock or at the hours shown, and need no appointment. The foundation does not provide round-the-clock crisis care — if the situation is acute, call them rather than filling in the form.

If someone near you is in danger: 103 — ambulance, 102 — police.

Articles

These articles do not replace a consultation and do not diagnose. They describe typical reactions and what helps most people — your case may differ, and that is an ordinary reason to get in touch.
