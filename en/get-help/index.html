Get help

Help is free. Fill in the short form and we will contact you.

All of the foundation's services are free for beneficiaries.

If you need help right now

These lines operate around the clock or at the hours shown, and need no appointment. The foundation does not provide round-the-clock crisis care — if the situation is acute, call them rather than filling in the form.

  • 7333

    Lifeline Ukraine — suicide prevention, 24/7

  • 1547

    Government hotline — domestic and gender-based violence, 24/7

  • 116 111

    National child helpline, daily 12:00–20:00

These phone numbers are real and in service at the hours shown.

The Quick exit button

The Quick exit button in the header of every page instantly opens a different site and removes the current page from the Back button. The keyboard shortcut is Shift+Esc. It does not delete your browser history: if someone checks it later, this site will be there. If that is a risk, open the site in a private window (Ctrl+Shift+N, or Cmd+Shift+N on a Mac) or clear your history by hand.

Demo build: the form validates but sends nothing. On the live site the request goes to the foundation by email. The emergency numbers above are real.

A made-up name or a pseudonym is fine — we do not check documents.

One way to reach you is enough: a phone number or an email address.

What happens next

  1. You send a request

    The short form next to this. It takes a few minutes.

  2. We contact you

    The way you chose: phone, email or messenger.

  3. A short conversation

    To understand what kind of help you need and which format fits — online, in person in Lviv, or a rehabilitation rotation.

  4. The first session

    We propose a concrete option and, if you agree, arrange a time.

If it turns out you need help we do not provide, we will tell you where to turn.

What it costs

Nothing. All of the foundation's services are free for beneficiaries. We never ask for payment, a deposit or a "fee to skip the queue". If anyone asks you for money on the foundation's behalf, it is fraud — please tell us.

Confidentiality

What you tell a psychologist stays between you and the psychologist. We use your data only to process your request and do not pass it to third parties without your consent. The only exceptions are those the law requires: an immediate threat to life, or information about violence against a child.

This is not an emergency service

We do not operate around the clock and we are not an emergency service. If you or someone near you is in danger right now:

  • 7333 — Lifeline Ukraine, suicide prevention, 24/7;
  • 1547 — domestic and gender-based violence, 24/7;
  • 116 111 — National child helpline, daily 12:00–20:00;
  • 103 — ambulance, 102 — police.

If being on this site is not safe

The Quick exit button is described above on this page. In short: it removes one step from the Back button, but it does not delete your browser history. If the device is shared, use a private window.
