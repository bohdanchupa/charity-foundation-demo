Online consultations supported by Stichting Vluchteling

A four-month grant for up to 10 free video consultations per person. The plan was exceeded: 3,346 consultations for 852 people.

Completed

Donor
Stichting Vluchteling
Period
2023-02-01 — 2023-05-01
Geography
  • All of Ukraine
  • Ukrainians abroad
Sector
Health / Mental Health, Protection
Programme
UNIQ.me — free psychological help

Key results

  • 3,346

    consultations, against a plan of 2,940

  • 852

    people helped, against a plan of 600

  • 98%

    positive feedback

  • 35%+

    continued therapy at their own cost after the free course

Goal

Up to ten free online video consultations for every Ukrainian affected by the war. The online format was a deliberate choice: it gives equal access whatever a person's location, age, sex or physical ability. Clients can choose the sex of their specialist.

How the plan was exceeded

Consultations came in 13.8% above plan and the number of people helped 42% above plan, with no increase in budget. Three decisions made that possible:

  • Two pay grades, one for psychologists and one for psychotherapists. More complex cases go to psychotherapists, the rest to psychologists.
  • A pace of about four consultations a month instead of the planned five.
  • Closing a case early when that is clinically appropriate. Specialists are encouraged to finish in fewer sessions when that is enough for the person. The time this frees goes to the next client.

Who came to us

  • Refugees abroad: 179 people (21%). Internally displaced people: 59 (7%).
  • 79% of requests came from women, and 95% of them asked for a female specialist. 82% of the platform's specialists are women.
  • Returnee status and disability were not formally tracked in this project. We do not quote figures we did not collect.

Risks and how we managed them

RiskWhat we did
Power outages40 kW generators bought with donor help; sessions rescheduled by mutual agreement
Military threatTeam relocated to western Ukraine and abroad, an evacuation plan, a reserve pool of specialists
CyberattackDatabase mirrored abroad, continuous backups, security certificates
Data leakEncryption, legal compliance review
Demand above capacitySelection criteria, a waiting list, reallocation of unused sessions

Feedback

After every session the client can leave feedback on their specialist. A moderator reads every response. When feedback is negative, the moderator contacts the specialist and, if needed, assigns the client someone else. There were few such cases, and all were resolved.

The platform's information line takes both routine and crisis calls. Crisis calls follow a documented algorithm for redirection to national services.
