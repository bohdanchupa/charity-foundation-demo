Catering services for rehabilitation rotations in the Carpathians

Three meals a day for eight ten-day rotations in 2027: approximately 40 people per rotation, accommodating participants' dietary restrictions.

OpenUCARE/2026/RFQ-014Deadline: 2026-10-10

Subject of the procurement

Catering services for eight rehabilitation rotations in the Carpathians during 2027. Estimated 40 participants per rotation (children and adults), each rotation lasting 10 days, three meals a day plus two snacks.

Estimated total volume: 9,600 person-days of catering.

Key requirements

  • valid documentation entitling the supplier to provide catering services, and HACCP compliance;
  • readiness to work from the kitchen at the Carpathian base (location given in the terms of reference);
  • menus accommodating dietary restrictions — gluten-free, lactose-free and vegetarian, for an estimated 10–15 % of participants per rotation;
  • a separate children's menu;
  • willingness for staff to complete the foundation's PSEA and child protection training before work begins — a mandatory condition, since staff share the site with children.

Evaluation criteria

CriterionWeight
Price50 %
Experience of catering at remote sites20 %
Capacity to provide dietary menus20 %
Timing and fit with the rotation schedule10 %

How to submit

Send the completed price quotation form together with copies of permits and a description of relevant experience to the address given in the terms of reference by 23:59 on 10 October 2026, marked "UCARE/2026/RFQ-014".

Questions on the tender documents are accepted until 3 October 2026. Answers are circulated to all bidders who submitted a question, without attributing the question.

Integrity

The foundation charges no fee of any kind for participating in a procurement. Any demand for payment or "facilitation" in the foundation's name is fraud — report it via the contacts in the Accountability section. A conflict of interest between a bidder and a member of staff must be disclosed and is grounds for rejecting the bid.
