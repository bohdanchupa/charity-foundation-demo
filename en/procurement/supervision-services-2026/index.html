Clinical supervision services for the foundation's psychologists

Group and individual supervision for 20 specialists working with trauma, over a 12-month period.

OpenUNIQ/2026/RFP-009Deadline: 2026-10-17

Subject of the procurement

Clinical supervision services for foundation specialists working with psychological trauma. Period: 12 months from January 2027.

Volume: monthly group supervision for four groups of five, plus individual supervision sessions on request — approximately 120 hours a year.

Why this is procured separately

Trauma work without supervision burns a specialist out within the first year. Supervision is funded as a ring-fenced budget line precisely so that it cannot be cut in favour of more consultations.

Requirements

  • supervisor qualification evidenced by appropriate certification;
  • at least three years of demonstrable experience supervising practitioners who work with trauma;
  • ability to supervise in person in Lviv and remotely for specialists in other cities;
  • willingness to give the clinical lead anonymised, aggregated feedback — without disclosing the content of individual sessions.

Confidentiality

The content of supervision sessions is not shared with the foundation's management. The supervisor provides only aggregated conclusions about the team's training needs. The exception is situations falling under the foundation's safeguarding policy; the procedure for those is set out in the terms of reference.

Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | |---|---| | Supervisor qualification and experience | 45 % | | Price | 30 % | | Methodology and format | 15 % | | Availability and schedule | 10 % |
