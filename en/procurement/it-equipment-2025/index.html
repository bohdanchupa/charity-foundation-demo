Procurement of IT equipment for online consultations

Twelve laptops, noise-cancelling headsets and backup power supplies for the online stream's specialists. This procurement is closed.

ClosedUNIQ/2025/RFQ-021Deadline: 2025-11-30

This procurement is closed. The contract was signed on 12 December 2025. The notice is retained for transparency.

What was procured

  • 12 laptops for the online stream's specialists;
  • 12 noise-cancelling headsets with microphones;
  • 12 portable power supplies rated for four hours of autonomous operation;
  • 6 mobile routers with a backup connection.

Why this configuration

An online consultation cannot break off in the middle of a difficult topic. Autonomous power and a backup connection are not a convenience here but a protocol requirement: losing the connection during a session where someone has just begun to speak about a traumatic event does harm.

Noise-cancelling headsets exist for the same reason — the specialist must hear even quiet speech, and the client must not hear extraneous noise.

Outcome

Four bids were received. The winner was selected on combined criteria: price (50 %), compliance with technical requirements (30 %), delivery time and warranty (20 %). The equipment was delivered in January 2026.
