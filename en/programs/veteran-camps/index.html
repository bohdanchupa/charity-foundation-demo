Veteran rehabilitation in the Carpathians

Psychological rehabilitation for veterans in Carpathian camps, combined with physical rehabilitation.

  • Health / Mental Health
  • Specialised rehabilitation

Draft. Compiled from the foundation brief. Needs confirmation and real figures before launch.

Problem

Coming back from war is not a single event but a long process in which psychological and physical condition are tightly linked. Splitting the services — a psychologist in one place, a physical rehabilitation specialist in another — makes a person walk the same road twice. Veterans often postpone asking for psychological help, so entering through physical rehabilitation works better.

What we do

  • individual psychological support;
  • support groups for veterans;
  • physical rehabilitation within the same rotation;
  • psychoeducation sessions for veterans and their families;
  • stress-reduction activities;
  • referral to specialised care.

Who it is for

Veterans and members of their families.

Donor sectors

Health / Mental Health — psychotherapy, psychoeducation, PSS. Health / Specialised rehabilitation — physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and assistive devices where required.

Results

  • Veterans who completed a rotation
  • Psychological and physical rehabilitation sessions delivered
  • Family members involved in the programme
