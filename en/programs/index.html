Sectors and activities

Draft. Compiled from the foundation brief. The “ready to implement” list needs the foundation’s confirmation.

How to read this page

Emergency humanitarian donors think in sectors. Below are the sectors and activities in that terminology, marking what the foundation delivers today and what it is ready to implement within a project.

Health — Mental Health (MHPSS)

Delivered today. Psychotherapy, psychoeducation and psychosocial support (PSS): individual and group sessions online and in person, support groups, psychoeducation sessions, stress-reduction activities, referral to specialised care.

Health — specialised rehabilitation

Delivered today within veteran rotations. Physical rehabilitation combined with psychological support. Ready to extend to physiotherapy and occupational therapy for people with disabilities, specific needs or injuries, including the provision of assistive devices.

Protection

PSS component delivered today. Psychosocial support for children and their parents or caregivers: group sessions for children, safe and child-friendly activities, psychoeducation sessions, support groups for parents, referral.

Ready to implement within a project:

  • awareness activities on Child Protection, Gender-Based Violence (GBV) and Legal Protection;
  • Case Management — Child Protection Case Management for children, GBV Case Management for adults;
  • cash assistance — Emergency Cash Assistance (ECA) and Recurrent Cash Assistance (RCA);
  • distribution of Dignity Kits for survivors of gender-based violence, primarily women and girls.

The primary target group for Protection is internally displaced persons (IDPs).

Shelter

In development. Building a permanent mental and physical rehabilitation space in the Carpathians.

EORE — Explosive Ordnance Risk Education

Ready to implement awareness work on the risks of explosive ordnance as a project component.

Food Security

Ready to implement distribution of dry food parcels for internally displaced persons as a project component.

Designing a project

The content of any project depends on the donor, its priorities, requirements and maximum budget. We are ready to build a proposal for a specific call for proposals — write to the partnership contact.
