Rehabilitation camps for children and parents

Psychological rehabilitation in a Carpathian camp for children together with their parents or caregivers.

  • Protection
  • Child Protection
  • Health / Mental Health

Draft. Compiled from the foundation brief. Needs confirmation and real figures before launch.

Problem

A child who has lived through shelling, displacement or loss rarely recovers apart from the family. The parents' state directly affects the child's, and parents often have no resource left to seek help for themselves. A multi-day stay away from the usual environment gives what a weekly outpatient session cannot: continuity, safety, and the family working together.

What we do

  • group sessions for children;
  • safe, child-friendly activities;
  • psychoeducation sessions for children and adults;
  • support groups for parents and caregivers;
  • activities that reduce stress and improve emotional well-being;
  • referral to specialised psychological or psychotherapeutic care when needed.

Who it is for

Children and their parents or caregivers — primarily displaced families, families from de-occupied and front-line communities, and military families.

Donor sectors

Protection — Child Protection, PSS for children and their parents or caregivers. Health / Mental Health — psychoeducation and psychosocial support.

Child protection

Work with children follows the foundation's child safeguarding policy. Photographs of children are published only with written consent and without identifying details.

Results

  • Children who completed a camp rotation
  • Parents and caregivers reached
  • Group sessions and psychoeducation sessions delivered
