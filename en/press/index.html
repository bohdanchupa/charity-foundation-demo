For media

A contact for journalists, a foundation profile in two lengths, the rules for using our material, and what we do not provide.

We reply within the working day. If your piece is urgent, say so in the subject line: we do not guess deadlines.

Foundation profile

50 words. Ucare Foundation works on the mental health of people affected by the war in Ukraine. Founded in Lviv in 2022. It provides individual and group psychological support online and in person, runs rehabilitation rotations for families in the Carpathians, and operates a mobile team in Kharkiv oblast.

200 words. Ucare Foundation was registered on 18 May 2022, three months after the start of the full-scale invasion. Its core was formed by psychologists already working with displaced families on a voluntary basis.

The foundation works across four streams: a mental health centre in Lviv with in-person consultations and therapy groups; online support for communities with no psychologist within reach; rehabilitation rotations in the Carpathians for children together with their parents; and a veterans stream of peer support groups led by a pair — a veteran facilitator and a psychologist.

Between May 2022 and August 2026 more than 22,000 people received support; 36 rehabilitation rotations were delivered; coverage reached 19 oblasts. The foundation is funded by institutional donors and by private contributions.

It also publishes a separate account of what it failed to achieve, with the figures and with what was done about each.

Using our material

No clearance needed: quoting published text from this site with attribution · using figures from the reports, citing the year and the document · downloading the logo to illustrate a piece about the foundation.

Clearance needed: any interview with a beneficiary · any photograph or video taken at a foundation site · use of the foundation's name in material where the foundation is a party to the dispute or the assessment.

What we do not provide

Beneficiaries' contact details. Never, under any circumstances, however firmly anonymity is promised. If a person wants to speak to the press they approach us themselves; we can pass your request to them, but not the reverse — and we do not tell the newsroom whether we passed it on.

Stories to fit a piece. We do not find a beneficiary to match a narrative that is already written.

Filming work in progress. There are no cameras in the consulting room, in a group, or at a rotation.

Comment on individual cases. Not even anonymised, and not even where the case is already public from another source.

If you are writing about suicide or trauma

Please follow the WHO guidance on reporting suicide: do not describe the method, do not present the act as a solution to a problem, do not publish notes left behind, and always include the contact details of support services.

This is not a formality. How a piece is framed has a measurable effect on imitation, and the editorial decision has consequences beyond the text.

We do not have a newsletter yet

This site is static and has no server that could take your address, and we will not hand it to a third-party service without a separate data processing agreement. For now there is an RSS feed — /en/feed.xml — which needs no sign-up and passes your details to nobody.
