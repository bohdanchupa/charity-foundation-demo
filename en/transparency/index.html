03

Safeguarding

Safety and dignity are the foundation of our work

We do not tolerate violence, exploitation, harassment, humiliation, discrimination or retaliation for reporting concerns. We pay particular attention to children and adults in vulnerable situations.

Support is never provided in exchange for money to a staff member or intermediary, services or acts of a personal nature.

Report a concernRead the safeguarding policy ↗

Ethics and resources

Zero tolerance for corruption and fraud

We do not tolerate bribery, misappropriation or hidden conflicts of interest. Funds must be used for stated purposes and supported by records.

Privacy

We request only necessary data

We explain why information is needed, how it is stored and who may access it. Consent to photos, video or personal stories is a separate decision, and refusal does not affect eligibility for support.
