Donors and partners

The organisations the foundation works with.

  • Northlight Humanitarian Fund

    Core donor of the psychosocial support stream since 2023. Funds the Lviv centre and the online support service.

  • Meridian Recovery Trust

    Supports the rehabilitation camps for children and parents, the 2024–2026 rotations.

  • Ostbrücke Stiftung

    Funds the veterans stream: peer support groups and retreat programmes for service members and their families.

  • Civic Resilience Initiative

    A small institutional grant for organisational capacity: policies, monitoring and audit.

  • ГО «Джерело Надії»

    Implementing partner in Kharkiv oblast: joint mobile team visits to front-line communities.

  • Коаліція «Крок»

    A network of four organisations with which the foundation shares a client referral pathway and joint supervision.

  • Міська програма «Разом»

    A cooperation memorandum with the city mental health programme: premises for the centre on concessionary terms and referrals from the social services.

  • Інститут прикладної психології «Смисл»

    Academic partner: methodological supervision, placements for psychology students and joint training events.
