Accountability and feedback

How to raise a complaint or give feedback

Anyone — a beneficiary, a community member, a contractor, a member of staff — can report misconduct by personnel, a problem with a service, or a suspicion of fraud.

  • Openly — through the report a concern form or by email to safeguarding@uniq.foundation.
  • Anonymously — the form does not require a name or contact details; this does not affect how the case is handled.
  • Through a third party — a complaint may be submitted by anyone on behalf of the person affected.

We acknowledge receipt, review the case and report the outcome where there is somewhere to reply. Reports of sexual exploitation or abuse, or of a threat to a child, are handled as a priority.

Retaliation is prohibited. No one loses access to the foundation's services because they raised a complaint or provided information.

Data protection

Data from the site forms is used only to process the request. We do not sell it or pass it to third parties without consent, except where the law requires. The site uses no advertising cookies and carries no consent banner, because it collects nothing that would need one: analytics are anonymous.

Accessibility

We work to WCAG 2.2 level AA and do not claim full conformance: claiming it without an external audit is a promise we cannot evidence.

Implemented and verified:

  • keyboard operation on every page, with a visible focus indicator;
  • motion disabled when the system asks for it — verified by measurement in a browser, not only from the code;
  • text alternatives for images;
  • the site stays readable with JavaScript off.

Known limitations:

  • no external accessibility audit has been carried out;
  • testing covered Chromium and WebKit; Firefox and screen readers were not systematically tested;
  • the PDF documents have not been checked for accessibility and are not tagged.

If you hit a barrier, write to us: we will fix it and add it to this list.
