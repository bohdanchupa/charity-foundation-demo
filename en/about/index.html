About the foundation

International charity foundation: online psychological support, a mental health centre in Lviv, and rehabilitation camps in the Carpathians for children, parents and veterans.

Mandate

To restore the mental health of Ukrainians, lost as a result of events connected with the war in Ukraine.

Our goal

To use our experience and our team to deliver projects that help Ukrainians restore their psychological health as quickly as possible and become a mentally healthy, strong nation.

Who we are

A team of civic activists, practising psychologists and psychotherapists, and researchers, delivering projects that help Ukrainians cope with the consequences of the war — for eight years, from the first days of the invasion.

What we do

Four ways of delivering the same help to people in different circumstances.

  • 01

    UNIQ.me — free psychological help

    A platform that matches a person with a qualified psychologist and covers the fee. More than 800 specialists and 32,000 consultations delivered.

  • 02

    Heroes — complex rehabilitation for people with combat experience

    A 21-day programme for demobilised soldiers in a state of combat stress, delivered residentially and as outpatient care by a multidisciplinary team.

  • 03

    Recovery programme — family retreats for mothers, fathers and children

    Free recovery camps in Lviv for families affected by the war, followed by individual online support on the UNIQ.me platform.

  • 04

    EQ.app — developing emotional intelligence

    The world's first app for developing emotional intelligence. Free for Ukrainians, with more than 200,000 downloads.

  • 05

    Institute for the Development of Emotional Intelligence

    Founded in 2021 at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv. Developing, implementing and popularising the concept of emotional intelligence.

  • 06

    All-Ukrainian Psychological Marathon

    An online marathon running three to four months, 20+ topics, more than 100,000 participants in Ukraine and abroad. Free access to every recording.

Values

  • The person is the subject of their recovery

    Not the object of assistance. The beneficiary decides the pace and the scope of the work.

  • Do no harm

    Every activity is designed so that it cannot cause re-traumatisation.

  • Confidentiality

    An approach and its content are not shared with third parties without the person's consent.

  • Free of charge

    Services are free to beneficiaries. No top-ups, no fees and no "voluntary donations" for an appointment.

  • Non-discrimination

    Regardless of sex, age, ethnicity, religion, disability or displacement status.

  • Child protection and PSEA

    Work follows the safeguarding and PSEA policies, both published in full.

Who we work with

  • Internally displaced people
  • Children and their parents or guardians
  • Veterans and their families
  • People affected by gender-based violence
  • Residents of de-occupied and front-line communities

How the foundation came about

  1. 2014

    "Mothers of the mobilised"

    Psychological rehabilitation centres for the families of the first front-line soldiers.

  2. 2016

    "Heroes"

    The methodology is developed; residential and outpatient care for demobilised soldiers.

  3. 2020

    EQ.app and a world patent

    The method for developing emotional intelligence as a factor in preventing PTSD.

  4. 2021

    The Institute of Emotional Intelligence

    Founded at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv.

  5. 2022

    A hub, the camps and the platform

    The largest displacement hub in western Ukraine, the launch of the recovery camps, and the help platform built with seven professional bodies.

  6. 2023

    Streams for children and for PTSD

    Psychological help for children, adolescents and families; psychotherapeutic support for people with PTSD.

  7. 2025

    The All-Ukrainian Psychological Marathon

    More than twenty topics, leading specialists, more than a hundred thousand participants.

How decisions are made

  • The supervisory board

    Five people. Approves the strategy, the annual budget and the reporting, and selects the external auditor. At least two members have direct personal experience of what the foundation works with.

  • The audit committee

    Chaired by a person with no financial relationship to the foundation outside that role. Any conflict of interest involving a board member or a member of staff must be disclosed.

  • External audit

    Annually, by an independent firm selected competitively. The opinion is published in full, including any qualifications.

What we do not do

This is not small print. Knowing the limit before you approach matters more than learning it afterwards.

  • We are not a medical institution

    We do not treat conditions requiring medication or hospitalisation — we refer, and we accompany the person through the referral.

  • We are not a round-the-clock crisis service

    That is what the helplines listed at the foot of every page are for.

  • We publish no story without consent

    And we do not use images that could identify a person, even where that would make the material more persuasive.
