This form has no consent checkbox so an anonymous report stays possible. We handle what you send here under our legitimate interest in safeguarding, and, where someone's life, health or safety may be at risk, to protect their vital interests.

Who may report

Anyone: a beneficiary, a family member, a member of the community, a member of staff, a volunteer, a contractor or an outside observer. You do not need to be certain — a suspicion is enough.

Where it goes

A report goes straight to the safeguarding focal point, bypassing line managers. That is deliberate: the previous route ran through the immediate manager, who is precisely the person most often complained about.

The safeguarding focal point will be named once the Foundation’s structure is approved. The official safe complaints channel is being prepared for publication — it will be listed on the Contacts page.

Anonymity

A name and contact details are optional. An anonymous report is treated exactly as a signed one. If you leave no contact details we cannot tell you the outcome — that is the only difference.

Protection from retaliation

Any measure taken in response against a person who reported in good faith is prohibited. This covers staff, contractors and beneficiaries alike: making a report does not affect access to the foundation's services.

What the foundation publishes

The number of reports received and handled, by category, is published in the annual report — without details that could identify the reporter. Four reports were received in 2025.

If there is immediate danger

This channel is not an emergency service. If there is a direct threat to life or health, call 102 (police), 103 (ambulance) or 1547 (domestic and gender-based violence, 24/7).
