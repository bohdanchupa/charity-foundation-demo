Monitoring and Evaluation Officer

Collecting and analysing results data, reporting against donor indicators, and developing the beneficiary feedback system.

OpenLocation: Lviv, hybridContract type: Full-time, employment contractDeadline: 2026-10-08

About the role

The foundation measures not only how many services were delivered but how the condition of the people who received them changed. This post is responsible for making sure that data exists, is reliable, and reaches the reports — including when it shows that something did not work.

You will work across all four programmes and directly with the finance director and the head of partnerships.

Responsibilities

  • maintaining a single indicator framework across programmes;
  • organising data collection: assessment at intake, at exit and six weeks later;
  • analysing data and drafting the results sections of the annual report;
  • reporting against donor indicators in their formats and to their deadlines;
  • developing the beneficiary feedback system, including its anonymous channel;
  • training colleagues to collect data correctly in the field.

Requirements

  • at least two years in monitoring and evaluation in a non-profit or international organisation;
  • confident work with data: spreadsheets, aggregation, basic descriptive statistics;
  • understanding of the ethics of collecting data on vulnerable groups — anonymisation, minimisation, consent;
  • English sufficient for donor reporting;
  • fluent Ukrainian.

An advantage: experience with standardised mental health assessment scales, and experience preparing reports for institutional donors.

What we offer

  • formal employment, hybrid schedule;
  • a training budget;
  • 24 calendar days of leave;
  • direct access to the leadership: your results are not softened before publication.

How to apply

CV and covering note through the form below by 8 October 2026. In the covering note, briefly describe an occasion when data you collected showed a negative result, and what the organisation did about it.
