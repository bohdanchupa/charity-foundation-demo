How to support the foundation

Three routes: a bank transfer, an institutional partnership, or professional help pro bono.

Why it matters

Almost all of the foundation's money is restricted grant funding. A grant pays for what is written into its budget and for nothing else. The transfer below is the foundation's only unrestricted income, and it is what pays for the things no application covers: supervision between projects, a twelfth session for a client whose grant funded ten, the journey to a community that was not in the budget in time.

This site does not process payments

This is a static site: there is no server and no checkout. The transfer is made in your own bank using the details below. We have deliberately not put up a button that pretends to take money.

What things cost

These figures are not a request for a particular sum. They are the answer to a question donors ask.

  • One individual consultation — about UAH 620, including the specialist's time, supervision and premises.
  • A full course of 10–12 sessions — about UAH 7,000.
  • One participant-day at a rehabilitation rotation — about UAH 1,900, including accommodation, catering, transport and the team.
  • One hour of supervision for a specialist — about UAH 1,400. It is the line donors fund least willingly and the one whose absence destroys a team fastest.

Institutional partnership

If you represent a foundation, a company or an institution, write to our head of partnerships. We send the full due diligence pack: statute, policies, financial statements, the auditor's opinion and a description of the monitoring system.

Contact: partnership@uniq.foundation

Professional help

The foundation accepts pro bono help where it does not involve working with beneficiaries: clinical supervision, legal advice, translation, monitoring and evaluation, audit.

Why there is no general volunteering route

We do not place untrained volunteers with people who have lived through trauma. Non-professional support does harm in this field, and no amount of goodwill changes that. If you want to help directly, the routes above are the way to do it.
