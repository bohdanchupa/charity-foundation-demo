Psychological help where there was none

Online across 19 oblasts, a centre in Lviv, a mobile team in Kharkiv oblast and rehabilitation rotations in the Carpathians. The first consultation is free and commits you to nothing.

Programmes

All programmes →

  • UNIQ.me — free psychological help

    A platform that matches a person with a qualified psychologist and covers the fee. More than 800 specialists and 32,000 consultations delivered.

    • Health / Mental Health
    • Protection
    Read more →

  • Heroes — complex rehabilitation for people with combat experience

    A 21-day programme for demobilised soldiers in a state of combat stress, delivered residentially and as outpatient care by a multidisciplinary team.

    • Health / Mental Health
    • Protection
    Read more →

  • Recovery programme — family retreats for mothers, fathers and children

    Free recovery camps in Lviv for families affected by the war, followed by individual online support on the UNIQ.me platform.

    • Protection
    • Child Protection
    • Health / Mental Health
    Read more →

  • EQ.app — developing emotional intelligence

    The world's first app for developing emotional intelligence. Free for Ukrainians, with more than 200,000 downloads.

    • Health / Mental Health
    Read more →

  • Institute for the Development of Emotional Intelligence

    Founded in 2021 at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv. Developing, implementing and popularising the concept of emotional intelligence.

    • Health / Mental Health
    Read more →

  • All-Ukrainian Psychological Marathon

    An online marathon running three to four months, 20+ topics, more than 100,000 participants in Ukraine and abroad. Free access to every recording.

    • Health / Mental Health
