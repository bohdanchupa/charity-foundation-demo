UNIQ International Charitable Foundation

Support that helps people move forward

We help Ukrainians affected by war care for their mental health, rebuild inner resilience and find the support they need. We pay particular attention to children, families and internally displaced people.

We bring together charitable resources, professionals and partners to design and deliver our own support programs.

01

Who we are

A Ukrainian charity for people and communities affected by war and other crises

Our priorities are mental health, inner resilience, and support for children, internally displaced people and people facing difficult life circumstances.

We mobilise donations and grants, develop our own programs and work with Ukrainian and international organisations.

Mission, vision and values
02

Focus areas

Support shaped around people and communities

The form of support depends on participants’ needs, program conditions and professional competence. When specialist care is needed, we arrange safe referrals.

01

Mental health and psychosocial support

Programs for people living with the effects of war, loss, displacement and prolonged stress.

02

Children and families

Children’s psychological wellbeing, development and social connection, with respect for their age, needs and views.

03

Internally displaced people

Adapting to new circumstances, rebuilding social ties and finding the support people need.

04

Difficult life circumstances

Support for people who have lost housing, loved ones, income or their usual support network, including older people and people with disabilities.

05

Community recovery

Local initiatives, mutual aid and partnerships that widen access to support.
